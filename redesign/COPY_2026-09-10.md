H1: Cody Peterson is a trickster.

Paragraph 1:
I got sober on a curb outside the Hard Rock in Las Vegas on March 4, 2013. I was thirty-four, homeless, with warrants in four states. I haven't had a drink or a drug since.

Paragraph 2:
Five or six years into sobriety I went back to books. Campbell first, then Jung, then Homer, who turned out to have an older language for the things I had been living through: feeling, endurance, the soul. That is what I write about now.

Paragraph 3:
I own a rain gutter company in Las Vegas and I still handle the sales. I study psychology at the University of Utah and start a master's at Adams State in January 2027. In between I build things: Seba.Health, a depth psychology library and directory, and Logoi, a workbench for reading Greek. My wife and I lived next to Zion for five years. I go back to the canyons whenever I can.
