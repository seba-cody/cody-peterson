AboutHenderson, Nevada

A working scholar & founder.

I write about depth psychology, antiquity, and addiction recovery from Henderson, Nevada. My research follows a single throughline — the thumos of the Greeks, the lost organ of feeling — across Homer, the Jungian tradition, and the contemporary science of interoception.

Outside the writing desk: founder of City Seamless (rain gutters, Las Vegas Valley) and 702 Alliance (locally-owned business directory). All three sites circle the same question — how a place, a person, or a feeling becomes itself again.
