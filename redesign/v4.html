Reading Homer through the lens of depth psychology.

I am a depth psychologist, author, and founder of Seba.Health. My work follows a single throughline — the thumos of antiquity, the ancient organ of feeling — across Homer, the Jungian tradition, addiction recovery, and the contemporary science of interoception.

My book The Shadow of a Figure of Light (Chiron Publications, 2024) reads the archetype of the alcoholic as a long shadow cast by Western culture's lost organ of feeling. My current research, in Jung Journal, follows the iron thumos of Odysseus into the consulting room.
